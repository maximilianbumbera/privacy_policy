# Privacy Policy for Time Travel Clicker and Toy Rampage

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

## Introduction

This privacy policy applies to the Android games Time Travel Clicker and Toy Rampage, developed by Crafterko. Time Travel Clicker is ad-supported. Live ads are disabled in the current Android version of Toy Rampage.

## Information Collected by the Games

**Time Travel Clicker:** The game itself does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information. It uses a timestamp to run its gameplay mechanics. The game does not keep or transmit that timestamp.

**Toy Rampage:** The game stores your chosen in-game name, settings, progress, coins, unlocks, and achievements on your device so you can continue playing later. The game does not send this saved data to the developer.

## Third-Party Services

Time Travel Clicker uses [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Unity Ads](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy). These services may process information such as device identifiers and ad interactions, as described in their own privacy policies.

The current Android version of Toy Rampage does not load live ads. Some buttons simulate ad rewards without contacting an ad network.

## Children’s Privacy

Time Travel Clicker is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the games. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the developer using the email address below.

## Opt-Out and Data Retention

Toy Rampage keeps game data on your device until you clear the app’s data in Android settings or uninstall the game. Android may back up or restore app data depending on your device settings.

Third-party services used by Time Travel Clicker may retain information according to their own privacy policies. See the links above for details and available privacy controls.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
